FAQs

What types of coaches benefit most from AI versions of themselves?

Coaches with established content, frameworks, and methodologies see the biggest impact from AI versions. Business coaches, executive coaches, life coaches, and consultants who regularly answer similar client questions or want to scale their expertise beyond one-to-one sessions find Coachvox particularly valuable. The platform works best for coaches who have developed their unique approach and want to make it available 24/7.

What is conversational AI for coaches and how does it work?

Conversational AI for coaches uses natural language processing to have meaningful dialogues with clients using your specific coaching methodology and expertise. Unlike basic chatbots that follow scripts, Coachvox creates genuine conversations that adapt to each client’s needs while maintaining your unique coaching style and approach.

Do I need any technical knowledge to create an AI coach?

No, you don’t need any technical knowledge or coding skills to create your AI coach with Coachvox and start scaling your impact.

How quickly can I build my AI version?

Very quickly! Upload your books, articles, transcripts to Coachvox and your AI learns your content.

Then add your bio and slide the style sliders, to create an AI version of you that your customers can chat with.

Chat to your AI before it goes live. You rate its responses and it learns your feedback for future conversations.  Many of our creators have their Coachvox live within a couple of hours.

How does AI coaching compare to traditional chatbots?

AI coaching tools like Coachvox are trained on your specific frameworks, content, and coaching philosophy to provide personalized guidance. Traditional chatbots follow predetermined scripts and can’t adapt to complex coaching scenarios. AI coaches understand context and provide responses that reflect your unique expertise and methodology.

What makes Coachvox different from ChatGPT for coaching?

ChatGPT provides generic responses based on general training data, while Coachvox creates an AI specifically trained on your content, methodology, and coaching style. Your Coachvox AI maintains consistency with your approach and can be embedded directly into your business systems for seamless client experiences.

Can my AI speak in different languages?

Yes. Your AI will answer questions in the language it’s asked, as long as that language is supported. Most commonly spoken languages work. If you’ve trained it in English, but it has questions in Spanish, for example, it will reply in Spanish but based on translations of your English training data.

Can I charge clients to access my AI coach?

Absolutely! With specialist tools like Coachvox you can set your price and charge a weekly or monthly subscription to your AI coach version. You can add trial days and coupon codes too to help with promotion.

How do I train my AI coach to sound like me?

Upload your best content including articles, books, session recordings, and client materials that showcase your coaching style. Use Coachvox’s style sliders to adjust personality traits, then chat with your AI and rate its responses to fine-tune how it communicates. The system learns from your feedback to better match your voice and approach.

How much content do I need to create my AI coach?

When adding content to Coachvox, focus on recency and relevance, not just quantity. You might need less than you think!

Your clients likely ask similar questions and want AI coaching on similar topics. Having excellent answers to these questions and solutions for common challenges is most important.

We mainly want your strong views, frameworks and unique worldview on your subject. That’s what your AI will refer to when engaging with clients.

Do I still own the copyright to my work?

Yes! You continue to own the copyright of the content that you use to train your AI, you simply grant us permission to use it in your model.

We store and protect your content carefully. The information you upload is used only for your AI model and is not used for any other Coachvox AI model or shared with any other platform.

How does it integrate across different platforms?

You can use your awesome Coachvox share page to get your AI out into the world or embed it on any page within your website or membership group.

Clients can add your AI to their mobile device’s home screen so you’re only ever a tap away.
