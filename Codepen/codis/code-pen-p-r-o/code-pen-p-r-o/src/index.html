How to measure

  • Chest: Measure under the arms at the fullest point of the bust
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waist, the narrowest point
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips while standing
  • Inseam: Measure from the top of your inner thigh to the bottom of your ankle bone while standing
