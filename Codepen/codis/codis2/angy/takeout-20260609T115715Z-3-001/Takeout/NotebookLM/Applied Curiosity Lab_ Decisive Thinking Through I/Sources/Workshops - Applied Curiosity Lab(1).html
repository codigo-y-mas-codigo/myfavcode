Workshops

Applied Curiosity Lab offers three engaging, interactive workshops : Applied Curiosity, Good Thinking Decision Framework and ** Critical Thinking.** All workshops are optimized for leadership teams ready to find new ways to capture insights, challenge assumptions, and make better decisions. Applied Curiosity

For Insights & Influence

Teams will gain new ways of using curiosity as they journey through Applied Curiosity Lab's interactive, thought-provoking case study with curiosity as their guide.

Learning Objectives

*  Tap into the power of Curiosity Archetypes
*  Spot and avoid Curiosity Killers
*  Differentiate between free-range and Applied Curiosity
*  Craft and ask the Most Valuable Questions (MVQs) to reveal insights and spark interest
*  Know when more curiosity is beneficial and when it's not
*  Use Peak Curiosity to ethically persuade
*  Take steps toward creating a durable Culture of Curiosity

Critical Thinking

The PIQPACC Framework

Teams will use ACL's critical thinking PIQPACC framework, while engaging in an interactive case study to improve thinking and make wiser decisions.

Learning Objectives

*  Differentiate between critical, strategic, and creative thinking
*  Define and set clear boundaries for your purpose
*  Assess information for RRV: reliability, relevance, validity
*  Identify and avoid thinking and judgment errors
*  Ask Most Valuable Questions (MVQs)
*  Communicate with clarity
*  Collaboratively engage with a mini case study to make a decision.

Sales & Marketing

Even the most reliable sales and marketing programs leave many personal and market insights undiscovered. Distracting information and the pace of change means that teams are rarely given time to think about their thinking. Applied Curiosity & critical thinking tools will make your team more self and customer-aware, more influential, more intellectually agile, and ready to capture otherwise hidden and meaningful market insights.

Finance & Investing

Unlocking potential requires bridging the gap between what you know and what you need to discover about your market and yourself. Our workshops will shine a light on misleading biases, logical fallacies, and unexamined assumptions through practical tools and strategies that can drive your business forward.

Creative

Is your talent trained, encouraged, and inspired to ask the best questions? Are they prepared to extract and share useful insights? We'll help you create a vibrant Culture of Curiosity where inquiry and questions are celebrated. Creative insights will flow freely and influence will soar.

Analytical & Technical

No matter how analytical or technical you are, we all lack an operating manual for our brains. This has profound implications and consequences for the effectiveness of our decisions. As you continue to improve upon algorithms and prescriptive models for making decisions, we'll give you the Good Thinking tools to unlock an operating manual for your brain and avoid judgment and decision errors that negatively impact algorithm decisions.
