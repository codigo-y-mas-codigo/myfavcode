# Shadow Play

A Pen created on CodePen.

Original URL: [https://codepen.io/atzedent/pen/GgNOaQG](https://codepen.io/atzedent/pen/GgNOaQG).

The shader captures the fleeting interplay of digital architecture and dynamic light. A luminous sphere pierces the darkness, transforming a rigid scaffold into a living, wandering web of light and shadow.

There are buttons at the top right, including one that lets you pause the timer. You can then use the mouse wheel to skip forward or backward one step at a time.
